# Business-Intelligence-in-Public-Transportation
The City transport bus data of Bhubaneswar City for the big data analyzation

This study examines how large, frequently real-time data sets are being generated, and how their analysis and aggregation might lead to new policy-relevant insights and operational improvements for transportation services and activities. It is divided into four pieces. The first section of the presentation gives an overview of the subjects that will be explored. The second discusses Big Data's creation, sourcing, and important features in Big Data analysis in general terms. The third part, business intelligence approach, discusses the data chosen for the case study and how to clean it for business intelligence visualization, as well as the significant pretreatment methods utilized prior to modelling and the modelling of the chosen dataset. discusses the legal frameworks that regulate data collection and usage, with an emphasis on privacy considerations for location data. The next section business intelligence solution discussing the evaluated result and the findings based on the report that created using Power BI.
The goal of this study is to perform a comprehensive investigation of big data and business analytics techniques for better corporate decision-making, technological approaches, applications, and open research concerns. Furthermore, the study intends to show the significant benefits that big data has provided to firms in developed countries, as well as how these benefits might be replicated by local enterprises. The paper also addresses the multiple difficulties confronting big data analytics, with a focus on data security, administration, characteristics, regulation, and compliances.
